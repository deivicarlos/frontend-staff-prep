# Career session

Read `career/CAREER.md` first. It holds the decisions and their status. Only open the two
`research/` files when a fact is needed.

## Resume and LinkedIn

- **Real title (Director), IC-first framing.** Every bullet is true and something he can
  defend in an interview. Improve the wording, never invent a claim or a number. Ask him for
  the real figure.
- **ATS-parseable:** a single column, standard headings (Experience, Skills, Education), real
  text (no text in images or tables), dates on every role, and his location stated as "Canada
  (remote)". Take keywords from real target postings he pastes, not generic lists.
- **Written in his voice, not a generic AI voice.** Specific systems, numbers and trade-offs;
  concrete verbs; varied bullet shapes. Cut stock phrasing ("spearheaded", "leveraged",
  "cutting-edge", "passionate", "results-driven") and the tricolon-plus-metric template repeated
  on every line. Give him a draft, and he rewrites the final wording himself. His edits are
  what make it his.
- The resume and LinkedIn must agree on title, dates and employers.
- Private files go in `career/private/` (gitignored).

## Why IC, and stories

- Build the "why IC" answer on the framing agreed in `CAREER.md`. Aim for about 60 seconds
  spoken, forward-looking, true. He rehearses it out loud and reports back where he stumbled.
- Stories: 2–3 in STAR form (Situation, Task, Action, Result), each converting a management
  year into Staff-level evidence (cross-team influence, a technical decision, mentoring). Each
  story's Result names something measurable.

## End

Update the status table in `career/CAREER.md` and the next item in `STATE.md`. Commit only
files outside `career/private/`.
